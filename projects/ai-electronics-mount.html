INDEPENDENT DESIGN PROJECT · PROPOSAL

PLANNED — NOT YET EXECUTED

AI-Assisted Design Optimization and Multiphysics Validation of a Lightweight Electronics Mount

Proposed goal: explore the tradeoffs between mass, stiffness, mechanical mounting constraints and dynamic response in a parametric electronics bracket, then validate selected predictions with physical prototypes.

CAD + Design Exploration

Parametric SolidWorks or comparable 3D models, candidate geometries, and documented design constraints.

Physics-Based Simulation

Static stress and deformation; eigenfrequency/modal analysis; optional thermal stress if software and scope permit.

Fabrication + Verification

Printed prototypes, controlled mechanical loading, and comparisons between predicted and measured behavior.

Proposed engineering requirements

CategoryInitial study assumption
ApplicationMounting bracket for a small electronics module or circuit board
Payload and interfaceIllustrative 150 g module mounted with four M3 fasteners, subject to revision
Structural loadsIllustrative 5g and 10g quasi-static load cases
Design objectiveReduce mass while meeting separately defined stress, deflection and stiffness criteria
AnalysisStress, deformation and modal properties; mesh-convergence checks
TestingPrototype mass and controlled-load deflection; optional vibration measurements

Proposed work plan

  1. Baseline: define requirements, mounting geometry, materials, load cases and reference CAD configuration.
  2. Exploration: use AI assistance and Python for documented parameter sweeps; verify generated code, dimensions and engineering assumptions independently.
  3. Simulation: compare baseline and alternative designs using FEA with justified boundary conditions and mesh refinement.
  4. Validation: fabricate selected configurations and compare measured results with analysis; explain discrepancies and limitations.

Planned evidence and deliverables

CAD assemblies and engineering drawings; a design-variable table; reproducible analysis scripts; structural and modal plots; prototype photos; a simulation-versus-test comparison; and a short technical report documenting design tradeoffs.
